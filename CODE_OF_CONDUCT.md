# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make CV Builder Web welcoming and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of differing viewpoints.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy: never post another person's resume, contact details, credentials, or private
  documents.
- Use project channels for their intended purpose.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized content, doxxing, unwanted
contact, deliberate disruption, or sharing private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when this policy is violated. Report abusive content through GitHub's reporting
controls. For private security matters, use the reporting path in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
